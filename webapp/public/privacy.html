🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Puffin Quest is built and operated by Green Up Labs. We care deeply about your privacy — and we keep things simple. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. What We Collect

When you create a Puffin Quest account, we collect:

  • Account info — your username and password (hashed and salted; we never store plaintext passwords).
  • Quest submissions — photos, text descriptions, and timestamps you submit when completing quests.
  • Location data (optional) — only when you explicitly choose to share GPS for City Challenges. You can play without sharing location.
  • Device info — basic browser/device type and screen size to ensure the app works correctly.
  • Usage data — pages visited, quests completed, and features used to improve the experience.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To run and improve Puffin Quest — track your progress, puffin, streaks, and rewards.
  • To power community features — Review Cove approvals, shared profiles, and leaderboards.
  • To verify City Challenge locations — GPS data is only used to confirm you visited a landmark.
  • To plant trees — we count approved green quests toward our tree-planting campaign.
  • To fix bugs and improve performance.

3. What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We do not run ads or share data with advertising networks.
  • We do not track you across other websites or apps.
  • We do not store precise GPS coordinates beyond what's needed for City Challenge verification.

4. Photos & Quest Content

Photos you submit for quests are stored securely. They may be seen by other puffineers during the Review Cove approval process. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use a session token stored in your browser's local storage to keep you logged in. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure, encrypted servers. We use HTTPS for all connections. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms. We regularly review our security practices.

7. Children's Privacy

Puffin Quest is designed to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent for optional data collection (e.g., location)

9. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Render — for hosting the application
  • Neon — for database hosting
  • Cloudinary — for image storage and optimisation

Each service has its own privacy policy. We only share the minimum data necessary for these services to function.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we'll let you know through the app. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
